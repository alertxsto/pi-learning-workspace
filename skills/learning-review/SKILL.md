# Recall and review

- Read the relevant progress, misconceptions and review notes. Do not ingest the entire wiki each session.
- Pick a due/weak concept; explain why briefly. If no schedule exists, ask what the learner wants to review.
- Run questions, learner answers, hints and retries in the TUTOR panel without exiting/reopening the workspace. Preserve current-step and assistance history; send it to the parent agent on exit. Inline tutor is tool-free; a new authored transfer task still needs the main-agent handoff.
- Ask for recall before showing the explanation. For practical skills, use a small new task rather than only definitions.
- Note assistance explicitly. Separate forgetting, conceptual misunderstanding, and tool/environment failure.
- Give precise correction, then an independent retry or transfer task.
- Update durable evidence, unresolved gaps and the next review. Use a simple clearly labeled schedule initially; do not claim this is a deterministic FSRS scheduler.
- Don't impose a universal quiz score or mastery label. Use task-specific success criteria and repeated evidence over time.
- Preserve the learner's actual answers; never fabricate their explanations.
- Review is launched during Pi sessions. Automatic notifications while Pi is closed are not implemented.
